# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Individual investors reviewing their own portfolios.

## Product Purpose

PandaSet helps individual investors understand portfolio performance, risk, holdings, and investment research, and explore hypothetical portfolio changes.

## Operating Context

The browser app is organized around four workflows: portfolio overview, risk and exposure, research, and a what-if lab.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Portfolio data and returns are illustrative. The frontend and demo API are not connected to live market data or brokerage services. Preserve visible sample-data and modeled-result labels.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the PandaSet name and panda mark. The user selected a warm light palette built around panda ink, warm white, and bamboo green.

## Evidence on Hand

The app contains sample portfolio holdings, modeled performance and risk calculations, research summaries, and hypothetical scenarios. No live financial feed or brokerage integration is present.

## Product Principles

- Keep sample figures and modeled results clearly identified.
- Make portfolio holdings, risk, and research understandable together.
- Let investors explore hypothetical changes without implying that they were executed.
