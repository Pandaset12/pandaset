## Grammar and gate

New grammar: companion field guide. Navigation is an in-flow masthead and two chapter jumps. A persistent illustrated companion bridges a title composition and an operable comparison. Sections alternate exposition, evidence, and an indexed list of tools. Ending is a full-width invitation with a small-print colophon. Bans: full-screen video, floating dashboard screenshots, horizontal rails, repeated feature cards, numbered acts, continuous flight, and scroll cues.

The eight defaults lose for specific reasons: filmic one-shot hides useful navigation; chaptered editorial excludes the mascot from the first viewport; live surface forbids the requested marketing introduction; continuous world invents irrelevant geography; typographic poster excludes the meaningful illustration; gallery is for collections rather than one product; split stage forces comparison across the whole journey; rhythmic cutlist is too hurried for portfolio comprehension.

Fingerprint gate: the resolved registry is empty. First build has no previous row to compare.
